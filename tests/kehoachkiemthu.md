# Ke hoach kiem thu

## Unit test hien tai
- Ma tran quyen Admin/Thu thu/Doc gia.
- Escape truong CSV co dau phay, dau ngoac kep va xuong dong.

## Test nghiep vu se bo sung theo checklist
- Sach: ISBN trung, them/xoa/tim, 4 truong hop quay AVL LL/RR/LR/RL.
- BST: danh sach in ra co ISBN tang dan.
- Doc gia: va cham hash, tim/xoa cap nhat dung.
- Muon-tra: sach het, doc gia khong ton tai, tra muon han, da tra truoc do.
- File: tep thieu, tep hong, du lieu rong, backup.
- CTDL nang cao: BFS/DFS, kiem tra ket qua va benchmark.
