# Ke hoach kiem thu

## Da cai dat va chay tu dong
- `test_tinh_nang.cpp`: ma tran quyen Admin/Thu thu/Doc gia va escape CSV co dau phay, ngoac kep, xuong dong.
- `test_tuan3.cpp`: AVL voi bon truong hop LL/RR/LR/RL; them, tu choi ISBN trung, tim, cap nhat, xoa, kiem tra can bang; BST duyet in-order tang dan.
- `test_tuan4.cpp`: Hash Table separate chaining; them nhieu doc gia, tu mo rong, kiem tra va cham, tu choi khoa trung, tim, cap nhat, xoa.
- `test_tuan5.cpp`: LinkedList them/tim/xoa; Dynamic Array tang suc chua/truy cap/xoa; ghi-doc file nhi phan phieu muon va lich su giao dich.

## Chua kiem thu (se lam o cac tuan sau)
- Muon-tra: sach het, doc gia khong ton tai, tra qua han, da tra truoc do, tinh tien phat.
- File: tep bi cat/hong, phuc hoi backup, dong bo du lieu khi chuong trinh tat dot ngot.
- Graph: BFS/DFS, goi y sach cung the loai.
- Benchmark: do thoi gian theo nhieu quy mo du lieu va xuat CSV de ve bieu do.
