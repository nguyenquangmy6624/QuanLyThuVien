# Thiet ke kien truc va CTDL

Tai lieu thiet ke chi tiet se duoc hoan thien trong Tuan 2.

Cac CTDL theo yeu cau hien tai: AVL, BST, Hash Table (separate chaining), danh sach lien ket don, mang dong, do thi danh sach ke (BFS/DFS), CTDL cay tu tim hieu (loai cu the can xac nhan voi giang vien) va file I/O. Khong tu dong coi Trie la yeu cau da chot; moi CTDL can co ly do lua chon, vi tri su dung va phan tich do phuc tap.
